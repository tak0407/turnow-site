turnow Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026.

Information handled by the app

turnow requires no account. The app does not automatically collect personal information or send it to the developer’s servers. It has no advertising or analytics tools.

Timer settings, progress, and the state needed to extend a completed timer are stored on your iPhone. Records from earlier installations are left in place and are not used. Device backups follow your iPhone backup settings.

Motion data is processed on your iPhone to determine its orientation. Local notifications and Live Activities follow your preferences and iPhone settings.

Email support

If you email tak9447@gmail.com, we use your email address and the information you send to respond.

Support website

This support site has no analytics tools or cookies of its own. GitHub hosts it and may process web requests under GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
