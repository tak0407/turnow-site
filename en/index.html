Getting started

Open turnow on your iPhone and hold it upright. Turn it and hold the new position for about one second to start a 25-minute or 50-minute Pomodoro, or the stopwatch. Turn it upright again to finish.

You can change focus and break durations in Settings. The app is free and requires iOS 26 or later.
